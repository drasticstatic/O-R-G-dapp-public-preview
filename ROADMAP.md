(the octagon made of ORG's artwork, the beliefs, marbled ink you can stir, and a wallet-connect gift).
This is where it's headed, in rough order. No dates.

## Next

- **Crypto giving, made real**, once ORG designates and verifies a treasury address. Until then the
  gift page says plainly it's a preview on a placeholder address.

## Stewardship and succession

So this work outlives any one person (today drasticstatic, Christopher W, holds every key to it):

- [ ] A home in ORG's organization, if ORG wants to keep it, with at least two owners from ORG's own people
- [ ] Publishing through org-owned automation (a GitHub App, or an org bot account), not a personal token
- [ ] A token register, a renewal runbook, and written hand-over steps
- [ ] Backups of the repository, on a schedule
- [ ] Wallet connect: ORG's own Reown project (a one-line `org.js` change on ORG's portal, `give.reownProjectId`; this preview's `NEXT_PUBLIC_WALLETCONNECT_PROJECT_ID` follows), before any treasury address is published
